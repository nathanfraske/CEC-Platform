## Ruling 1 (Q1): Seed traces → hybrid with register tags; gate of record on the real register; analyst traces from the license-clean binding

**Decision.** Option (c), structured. Every seed case carries `register: reconstructed |
real`. Reconstructed traces (authored from the recorded audit findings) are legitimate
and cheap, and they measure the wrong distribution if treated as the gate: real analyst
output is 30 to 60 KB of meandering, and an extractor certified only against clean
reconstructions is certified for a world it will never see. So the **gate-of-record
metric computes on the real register only**; reconstructed cases serve as structural
smoke coverage and dev material.

Real traces are generated now by running the **license-clean analyst binding
(Qwen3.5-397B)** over the two audit contexts (boards plus known defects) and
hand-labeling gold tables against the harvested reasoning. The existing 2026-06-09 M2.7
bench traces may enter only as a **non-gating robustness register**, reported separately,
pending the owner's CL-20 bench-experimentation ruling: eval traces gate a binding,
which is load-bearing enough that a license-flagged model's output should not carry it
by default.

**Ratification cases: yes, now.** The seed includes RB-03 forced-choice cases derived
from the same traces: at least two conclusions-sourced cases (the skip path, asserting
ratification does not fire), at least two outside-sourced cases with the
deterministically perturbed distractor (polarity flip or locus swap, generated by
template, never by the extractor), and at least two no-conclusion traces asserting the
extractor returns `no_conclusion` rather than synthesizing (the CL-15 verify item folds
into this eval permanently).

**Verify.** Eval report shows per-register fidelity separately; the gate record cites
the real-register numbers; a ratification case with the distractor selected fails; a
no-conclusion trace answered with a verdict fails.

## Ruling 3 (Q3): Spans are quoted strings, normalized-exact, zero fuzz, case-sensitive; one verifier implementation shared by eval and production

**Decision.** The agent's recommendation is confirmed and made precise. Spans are quoted
strings, never character offsets (offsets die on any trace re-rendering). The match rule,
specified exactly: Unicode NFC normalization, runs of whitespace collapsed to a single
space, leading and trailing whitespace trimmed, **case preserved** (case carries meaning
in refs and net names; a case-folding match would let the extractor quote altered
identifiers), then exact substring containment. Zero fuzz: fuzz is where hallucination
hides.

Two field-class refinements. Prose spans (`basis_spans`, `evidence_spans`, mechanism
support) must be at least 20 normalized characters, because a five-character span
"existing" somewhere in 60 KB of trace is vacuous. Locus identifiers (refs, nets) are
exempt from the length floor and instead satisfy two checks: the identifier appears in
the trace as an exact token, and it resolves against the board facts through the shared
resolver.

**One implementation.** The verifier is a single module; the eval harness and the CL-15
production path both import it. The eval certifying a different rule than production
enforces is the failure the agent named, and shared code is the only reliable cure.

**Verify.** A shared-import test asserts eval and production call the same function
object. Property tests: whitespace-mangled quote passes, case-mangled quote fails,
19-character prose span fails, locus token resolving against facts but absent from the
trace fails.

## Ruling 5 (Q5): Hallucination defined as unsupported, truth irrelevant; gate fails per model-plus-prompt manifest with a full per-case report

**Decision.** A hallucinated verdict is any verdict or finding whose required spans fail
Ruling 3 anywhere in the trace. **Correct-but-unsupported counts as hallucination**, and
the agent's argument is exactly the framework's: fidelity is the property under test;
truth is settled elsewhere in DF-07 currency; an extractor producing right answers the
analyst never stated is doing the analyst's job, which is the precise failure RB-03
exists to catch. Zero-tolerance classes, any one of which fails the gate: hallucinated
verdict, span-not-found, and synthesis on a no-conclusion trace. Scored class: field
accuracy on the real register, proposed starting bar 90 percent, owner-settable in the
gate record.

Failure granularity: the gate is per model-plus-prompt manifest (CL-14 gates were always
per binding), so one hallucination fails **that candidate binding** with a complete
per-case report, and it neither fails other manifests nor blocks unrelated work.

**Verify.** A seeded correct-but-unsupported case fails the gate; the report names the
case, the field, and the missing span; a second manifest evaluated in the same session
is unaffected.

## Ruling 7 (Q7): Anchors are (a) plus (b); reject (c)'s red-as-steady-state and replace it with a calibration latch; 2152 numbers enter as Class A staging entries

**Decision.** Build (a) self-consistency anchors (dt_ipc reproduces IPC-2221 chart
points exactly; pure regression value) and (b) conservatism assertions (2221-computed dT
at or above the IPC-2152 reference on chart-domain points). Option (b) matters more than
it looks: it converts AM-04's "uncalibrated" label from a disclaimer into a **tested
property**, since the engineering claim of the uncalibrated solver is "conservative
until calibrated" and now CI proves the direction.

**Reject (c) as specified.** Goldens red by design trains everyone that red is normal,
which is the silent-gate lesson from the project's own learnings inverted into alarm
fatigue. The flip-forcing function (c) wanted is achieved with the **wired-and-latched
pattern from CL-03 Ruling 5**: the 2152 reference points land as data now, the runner
asserts conservatism against them while the family's calibration state is
`uncalibrated`, and the same points automatically tighten into accuracy assertions
(within an owner-set tolerance band) the day the family's calibration flips per Ruling
9. Green today, automatically stricter at calibration, never red by design.

**Source channel: Class A staging corpus entries**, standard-sourced with full
citations, never test-local constants, which would be corpus knowledge living outside
the corpus (CL-03 Ruling 1's rejection, again). Transition mechanics: while promoted/ is
empty, the golden tests read values from the entry files directly by entry id, so the
tests do not depend on promoted-only binding; at promotion the same entries also compile
through the params channel, and single source of truth holds throughout.

**Verify.** Chart-point anchors green; conservatism assertions green on every
chart-domain point; a synthetic calibration flip in a test tightens the same points to
the tolerance band; the 2152 entries lint clean with resolvable standard citations.

## Ruling 9 (Q9): Proposal confirmed with one correction: calibration is per family AND quantity; posture stays blocking-with-the-mark

**Decision.** `ThermalResult.calibration: "uncalibrated" | "bench:<label-ref>"`,
stamped into every thermal `Flag.detail`, riding cec_score detail, golden output, and
the corpus-fit sidecar: confirmed as proposed. Family vocabulary is the cec_facts
`families` dimension, the same one CL-03 Ruling 6 added, one vocabulary everywhere per
CL-23. **The correction:** calibration flips per (family, quantity), never per family
alone. A bench label for hotspot temperature flips hotspot calibration for that family
and says nothing about DC-IR; CL-13 labels carry the quantity they measured and the
latch keys on both.

**Posture: blocking with the mark, confirmed against the temptation.** `Flag.binding`
encodes **authority** (does this check have the standing to block, which staging never
does); `calibration` encodes **accuracy** (has reality vouched for the number). They are
different axes and they never trade. The uncalibrated solver is conservative, Ruling 7
now tests that conservatism every CI run, and a conservative gate blocking is exactly
the cautious posture the framework wants adjacent to spend. Demoting uncalibrated
thermal to advisory would convert an honesty label into an authority downgrade, which is
the precise conflation the binding field was built to prevent.

**Verify.** An uncalibrated thermal violation still blocks signoff and its detail
carries the mark; a synthetic hotspot label flips hotspot assertions for that family
while DC-IR stays uncalibrated; the latch state appears in the bundle.
