The problem you've had

Random reboots. Crashes under load. Fine in benchmarks, dead in the game you play. So you swap the power supply, reseat every cable, and hope.

It's almost always a power problem, it happens down at the connector, and no software can see it. So people buy parts on a hunch and lose a weekend finding out.

CEC is a flight recorder for your PC's power.

The two questions everyone asks

"Why not just take it to a shop?"

A shop can't see an intermittent power fault either. They swap parts to fix it, the same guessing you'd do. CEC hands over the recording, so the fix is known, not a hunch.

"Isn't the free monitoring software the same?"

No. That software reads your power supply's own sensors, can't see the graphics connector pins, and dies the moment the machine crashes. CEC measures the real thing and keeps it.
