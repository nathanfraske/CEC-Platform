Why it happens

One pin on the graphics-card cable quietly carries more current than the rest. It heats up. And nothing inside a normal PC is watching for it — until it melts.

It's not just cables. Power supplies wear out silently. Connectors work loose. A part can cook for weeks before it finally dies — with no warning at all.
