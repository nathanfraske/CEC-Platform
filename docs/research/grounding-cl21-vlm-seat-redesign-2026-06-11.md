# Grounding CL-21 and Redesigning the VLM Seat: VLM Geometric Blindness and Reference-Based PCB Inspection

## TL;DR
- **Head one is fully grounded.** CL-21 — "VLM seats are usable for selection, comparison, and structure/text reading, and NOT for absolute geometric measurement or counting; deterministic checkers own measurement" — is corroborated by strong peer-reviewed literature. CEC's specific four-board failure maps exactly onto two named, published failure modes: (1) low-level geometric blindness (BlindTest, MMVP) and (2) "blind faith in text"/sycophancy — the model parroting a fed-but-wrong rule and misquoting fed numbers ("islands > 1" when facts said islands = 1).
- **Head two answer is design-changing.** For CEC's synthetic, pixel-registered render pairs, the evidence says demote the VLM out of the primary detection path. A deterministic image-diff (XOR/subtraction) plus morphology pre-pass, augmented by KiCad 10 DRC and CAM/DFM checks, should own detection of every enumerable defect class; reference-based subtraction reaches sub-0.2% error precisely when registration is exact — which CEC has by construction. The VLM keeps a narrow but genuine residual: narrating flagged diff regions and an open-ended anomaly pass for unknown-unknowns (toolchain/renderer bugs).
- **The owner's hunch is partly right.** Eyes add real value for renderer/toolchain bug detection, human-readable triage, and unknown-unknown surfacing — but NOT as a measurer, gate, or primary detector. Roles 1 and 2 as originally framed are better served by deterministic diff/morphology; role 3 (anomaly surfacing) plus narration is the defensible VLM job, with the safety envelope (never gates, every flag re-checked) retained.

## Key Findings

### Head one: the published record on VLM geometric blindness and text-prior dominance

**1. VLMs fail elementary geometric perception and counting.** On BlindTest — 7 simple tasks (counting line intersections, circle overlap, identifying a circled letter, counting circles in an Olympic-like logo, nested squares, grid counting, path following) — Rahmanzadehgervi, Bolton, Taesiri & Nguyen ("Vision language models are blind," ACCV 2024, arXiv:2407.06581) report four SOTA VLMs averaging **58.57%** accuracy, the best (Claude 3.5 Sonnet) at **74.94%**, against human-expected **100%** and a random baseline of **24%**. (Note: the latest arXiv revision reports slightly different headline figures — **58.07%** average / **77.84%** best — because of model/version updates; both are in the record. Cite the version used.) The per-task lows are the load-bearing numbers for CEC: line-intersection counting averaged ~**56.84%** (GPT-4o ~**41.61%**), counting overlapping shapes (pentagons) ~**30.99%**, and grid counting ~**47.35%**. The authors' linear-probing experiment is decisive: the vision encoder contains enough information to solve the tasks, but the language model fails to decode it. These are exactly the "count discrete features / detect crossings / detect fragmentation" operations CEC asked its VLM to perform — and the literature predicts failure.

**2. VLMs default to language priors over visual evidence — the "blind faith in text" / sycophancy line.** Deng et al., "Words or Vision: Do Vision-Language Models Have Blind Faith in Text?" (CVPR 2025, arXiv:2503.02199), coined the term: when text contradicts the image, VLMs disproportionately trust the text. Corrupted text degraded some models by ~50% — Qwen2-VL-7B's accuracy on VQAv2/DocVQA/MathVista fell to **59%/63%/52%** of baseline (their Table 2; they also note Claude-Sonnet was most robust to corrupted text). This is the precise mechanism behind CEC's VLM "confirming islands > 1" against fed facts. The ancestry: the VQA-CP language-prior line (Agrawal et al., "Don't Just Assume; Look and Answer," CVPR 2018) showed VQA models answer from question priors alone; and POPE (Li et al., "Evaluating Object Hallucination in LVLMs," EMNLP 2023, arXiv:2305.10355) showed LLaVA, mPLUG-Owl and MultiModal-GPT answer **"Yes" near 99–100%** of the time regardless of image content (Table 3 "Yes %"), yielding ~50% accuracy despite ~99% recall — a yes-bias driven by frequency/co-occurrence text priors (≈half of hallucinated objects are top-10 frequent COCO objects; >half are top-10 co-occurring). The ICLR 2025 VLM-sycophancy study found VLMs "blindly agree with the user, contradicting the facts in the image," and — alarmingly — that sycophancy worsens as model size increases.

**3. What VLMs ARE reliably good at — the positive half of CL-21.** Tong, Liu, Zhai, Ma, LeCun & Xie ("Eyes Wide Shut?", CVPR 2024, arXiv:2401.06209) confirm the blindness via MMVP (GPT-4V **38.7%**, humans **95.7%**, random **25%**; all models except GPT-4V and Gemini scored below random), but the evaluation literature is clear that comparison/selection is more reliable than absolute judgment. The LLM-as-judge literature has a robust, replicated result: **pairwise comparison is more stable and human-aligned than absolute scoring**, because the judge makes a relative decision rather than calibrating an abstract scale. Zheng et al. ("Judging LLM-as-a-Judge with MT-Bench and Chatbot Arena," NeurIPS 2023, arXiv:2306.05685) found GPT-4 judges reach **>80% agreement with humans — the same level humans agree with each other** — with pairwise/Arena the most reliable mode; Liusie et al. (EACL 2024) corroborate that comparative assessment is more stable than absolute scoring. This directly supports framing any retained VLM role as pairwise-against-a-reference (role 2) rather than absolute "is this pour intact" (role 1).

**4. Mitigations and the established alternative — tool delegation.** The architecturally established answer is program-aided / tool-augmented visual reasoning: ViperGPT (Surís, Menon & Vondrick, ICCV 2023, arXiv:2303.08128) and Visual Programming (Gupta & Kembhavi, CVPR 2023 best paper) delegate measurement and counting to deterministic modules/code, using the LLM only to orchestrate. CEC's architecture — deterministic checkers own measurement, VLM orchestrates/narrates — *is* this pattern, independently arrived at. Critically, feeding ground-truth text alongside the image does NOT reliably fix visual attention; the blind-faith-in-text work shows it can make matters worse, because the model leans on the text. CEC's instinct to "provide the deterministic facts as context" backfired for exactly this documented reason.

### Head two: reference-based PCB optical inspection and the defect-class-to-method mapping

**1. Classical AOI and the golden-reference method.** Moganti, Erçal, Dagli & Tsunekawa, "Automatic PCB Inspection Algorithms: A Survey," *Computer Vision and Image Understanding* 63(2):287–313, 1996, is the anchor. It classifies inspection into **referential** (image comparison / golden-template, including image subtraction), **non-referential** (design-rule verification), and **hybrid** approaches. The canonical bare-board defect taxonomy is six classes — **open, short, mousebite, spur, pinhole, spurious copper (sliver/excess)** — codified by the DeepPCB dataset (Tang et al., "Online PCB Defect Detector On A New PCB Defect Dataset," arXiv:1902.06197). Subtraction/XOR comparison (align → binarize → XOR → threshold → morphology) works well precisely when registration is exact and lighting controlled, and is "highly sensitive" to misregistration and illumination otherwise — the dominant failure mode in physical AOI. On DeepPCB, the reference-based pipeline (SURF + adaptive threshold + XOR + morphology) achieves **detection error rates < 0.2% for all defect classes**.

**2. Modern deep-learning AOI and unsupervised anomaly detection.** Tang et al.'s YOLO-style detector on DeepPCB reaches per-class AP of **open 98.5%, short 98.5%, mousebite 99.1%, spur 98.2%, spurious copper 98.5%, pin-hole 99.4% (mAP 98.6)**, versus an image-processing baseline mAP of 89.3 and Faster R-CNN 97.6 (their Table 1). Unsupervised industrial anomaly detection (the MVTec AD line: PaDiM, ICPR 2021; PatchCore, Roth et al., "Towards Total Recall in Industrial Anomaly Detection," CVPR 2022, arXiv:2106.08265) needs only normal examples; PatchCore reaches **image-level AUROC up to 99.6% (ensemble), ~99.1% baseline — more than halving the error of the next best competitor**. **The decisive finding for CEC:** these learned methods exist to solve problems CEC does NOT have — camera noise, lighting, perspective, registration error, surface reflectance, and unseen defect morphology. When registration is perfect (CEC's synthetic, pixel-registered render pairs from the same kicad-cli SVG toolchain), plain differencing plus morphology is sufficient for every enumerable class; the learned methods' advantage is robustness to imaging variance that CEC's pipeline eliminates by construction. Nothing in this literature suggests learned methods beat plain image differencing when registration is exact.

**3. CAM/DFM deterministic checks for CEC's named gap classes.** Sliver detection, acid-trap detection, and minimum-feature checks are standard, deterministic, enumerable CAM/DFM checks (CAM350, DownStream DFMStream advertise exactly "Acid Traps, Copper Slivers, and Soldermask Slivers" with automatic repair). **KiCad 10 natively covers**: copper slivers (`DRCE_COPPER_SLIVER`, with a configurable sliver-angle tolerance), copper-to-copper clearance, minimum width/connection width, copper-to-edge clearance, isolated/dead copper ("Remove dead copper" in zone properties), via-count and diff-pair constraints, plus user-defined custom DRC rules. **Acid traps** (acute interior copper angles that trap etchant) are **NOT** a native KiCad DRC class — practitioners catch them via dedicated DFM tools or by writing custom DRC rules / increasing minimum zone width. This is the one named gap class that requires a deliberate addition to CEC's KiCad-based stack.

**4. Recommended pipeline shape for CEC (evidence-led):** **deterministic diff pre-pass → morphology → DRC/CAM checks (incl. custom acid-trap rule) → VLM narration of flagged regions + open-ended anomaly pass.** The VLM never measures and never gates; every flag is re-checked deterministically.

## Details

### Head-one corpus-entry candidates for CL-21 (statement form, each with citation set)

**CL-21.0 — the core principle.** VLM seats are reliable for selection, pairwise comparison, and structure/text reading, and unreliable for absolute geometric measurement or counting; deterministic checkers must own measurement.
- Rahmanzadehgervi, Bolton, Taesiri, Nguyen, "Vision language models are blind," ACCV 2024, arXiv:2407.06581 **[T1]** — BlindTest: 4 SOTA VLMs avg 58.57% (best 74.94%) vs. human 100%, random 24%; counting/intersection tasks worst; linear probing shows the encoder sees but the LM fails to decode.
- Tong, Liu, Zhai, Ma, LeCun, Xie, "Eyes Wide Shut?", CVPR 2024, arXiv:2401.06209 **[T1]** — MMVP: GPT-4V 38.7%, human 95.7%, random 25%.

**CL-21.1 — text-prior dominance corollary.** When prompt text asserts something the image contradicts, VLMs tend to repeat the text rather than read the image; feeding facts alongside the image does not reliably fix this and can worsen it. CEC's "data confirms islands > 1" hallucination is this mode.
- Deng et al., "Words or Vision: Do VLMs Have Blind Faith in Text?", CVPR 2025, arXiv:2503.02199 **[T1]** — corrupted text drops accuracy ~50% (Qwen2-VL-7B to 59/63/52% of baseline).
- Li et al., "Evaluating Object Hallucination in LVLMs" (POPE), EMNLP 2023, arXiv:2305.10355 **[T1]** — "Yes" bias near 99–100%; co-occurrence-driven hallucination.
- VLM sycophancy study, ICLR 2025 **[T1]** — VLMs blindly agree with user against image facts; worsens with size.
- Agrawal et al., VQA-CP, CVPR 2018 **[T1]** — language-prior ancestry.

**CL-21.2 — the prompt-rule trap corollary.** A fed heuristic that hard-codes the answer (e.g., "foreign crossings > 0 ⇒ clipped") will be parroted regardless of the image; the prompt itself predetermines the verdict. This is a prompt-design failure compounding text-prior dominance, and was mechanism (1) of CEC's failure.
- Same blind-faith and POPE citations; plus the BlindTest finding that many answers are inferable from text/choices alone (Rahmanzadehgervi et al., §1).

**CL-21.3 — pairwise-beats-absolute corollary.** Where a VLM is used at all, frame it as pairwise comparison against a frozen reference, not absolute scoring — pairwise / reference-anchored judgments are more stable and more human-aligned.
- Zheng et al., "Judging LLM-as-a-Judge with MT-Bench and Chatbot Arena," NeurIPS 2023, arXiv:2306.05685 **[T1]** — GPT-4 judges >80% human agreement; pairwise/Arena most reliable.
- Liusie et al., "Comparative Assessment of Text Generation," EACL 2024 **[T1]** — pairwise more stable than absolute scoring.

**CL-21.4 — tool-delegation pattern.** The correct architecture delegates measurement/counting to deterministic tools and uses the model only to orchestrate and narrate — exactly CEC's existing design.
- Surís, Menon, Vondrick, "ViperGPT: Visual Inference via Python Execution for Reasoning," ICCV 2023, arXiv:2303.08128 **[T1]**.
- Gupta & Kembhavi, "Visual Programming: Compositional Visual Reasoning Without Training," CVPR 2023 **[T1]**.

### Head-two defect-class-to-method matrix

| Defect class | Primary method | KiCad 10 native? | VLM role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stale-fill divergence (render vs. facts) | Deterministic cross-check of render against authoritative facts | N/A (CEC checkers) | Narrate only |
| Island / fragmentation (pour clipped) | Deterministic island count per net (CEC checker) + diff | Yes (connectivity/zone) | None — checker gates |
| Sliver (thin copper wedge) | DRC sliver check + morphology (erosion) | **Yes** (`DRCE_COPPER_SLIVER`) | Narrate |
| Acid trap (acute interior angle) | DFM/CAM check or custom DRC rule + min-zone-width | **No** (needs DFM tool/custom rule) | Narrate; flag candidates |
| Unexpected keepout | Deterministic diff vs. reference | Partial | Narrate |
| Corridor anomaly | Deterministic diff + morphology | No | Surface / anomaly pass |
| Wrong-net fill | Deterministic net-assignment / connectivity check | Yes (connectivity) | None |
| Open / short | Diff + connectivity | Yes | None |
| Mousebite / spur | Diff + morphology | Partial (width) | Narrate |
| Pinhole / spurious copper | Diff + morphology / dead-copper removal | Yes (dead copper) | None |
| Unknown-unknown (toolchain/renderer bug) | — no deterministic check exists | No | **VLM open-ended anomaly pass — the genuine residue** |

**The honest residue:** once the deterministic pre-pass exists, the only thing genuinely left for open-ended visual perception is the unknown-unknown class — failures no checker was written for, especially renderer/toolchain bugs where the render itself is wrong (e.g., kicad-cli exports a malformed SVG). Everything enumerable is better owned by diff + morphology + DRC/DFM. CL-21 and the AOI literature agree: the VLM's value is semantic/triage (narration, classification, novelty), not detection or measurement.

### Eval-design implications (the planted-defect battery)

- **Defect classes to plant:** the six classical bare-board classes (open, short, mousebite, spur, pinhole, spurious copper/sliver) plus CEC's named gap classes (stale-fill divergence, acid traps, unexpected keepouts, corridor anomalies, wrong-net fills). Plant each at varying sizes **down to the minimum feature**, because detection difficulty scales with the defect-to-pixel ratio (USPTO inspection prior art and the AOI surveys are explicit that the smallest detectable defect is set by registration/intensity error).
- **Render-only control is standard practice — include it.** Add a fully-intact board (the very control the original 4-board test had, and which the VLM wrongly flagged as clipped). A clean-vs-clean reference pair measures the false-positive rate; AOI literature routinely reports false-alarm rate alongside catch rate.
- **Catch-rate reporting:** report **per-class detection rate (recall)** and **false-positive rate**, mirroring the AOI convention (DeepPCB per-class AP ~98–99%; reference-method error <0.2%). For CEC: first report the deterministic pre-pass's per-class catch rate, then measure the **incremental** catch rate of the VLM anomaly pass on top. That incremental number is the only figure that justifies keeping eyes in the loop.

### The verdict on the owner's hunch (no flattery)

- **Where eyes genuinely add value (keep):** (a) **renderer/toolchain bug detection** — if kicad-cli exports a wrong SVG, the facts and the render diverge in a way no fact-based checker catches, and a VLM narrating "this region looks unlike anything the facts describe" is a real cross-modal sanity check; (b) **human-readable triage narration** of diff regions, which deterministic tools do not produce; (c) **open-ended anomaly surfacing** for classes no one enumerated. These are role 3 plus narration. The industrial-VLM literature (VELM/"Detect, Classify, Act," arXiv:2505.02626; AnomalyGPT, AAAI 2024) independently supports exactly this division of labor: a deterministic/feature-based detector decides *whether* something is anomalous, and the LLM/VLM decides *what it is* and *whether it matters* (distinguishing true defects from benign design updates) — classification and narration value, not detection value.
- **Where eyes do NOT add value (demote):** role 1 (consistency auditor as an absolute judge) and role 2 (reference comparison as the judge) are both better done by deterministic image-diff + morphology in the pixel-registered synthetic case — subtraction is near-perfect under perfect registration, and the VLM is unreliable at the very geometric primitives (counting, intersection, fragmentation) involved (BlindTest/MMVP). The VLM should *narrate* the diff output, not *produce* it.
- **Cost side:** added latency (~1.1–1.3 s/call as observed) and VRAM for the multimodal/mmproj seat, plus the maintenance burden of another seat. Justified only if the incremental unknown-unknown catch rate over the deterministic pre-pass is measurably > 0 on the planted battery.

## Recommendations

1. **Immediately (no new model work).** Build the deterministic diff + morphology pre-pass against the frozen known-good reference render (align → XOR/subtract → threshold → morphological open/close → connected-component extraction). Wire in KiCad 10 DRC (sliver, clearance, min-width/connection-width, dead-copper) and **add a custom DRC rule or a DFM step for acid traps**, which KiCad does not check natively. This owns all enumerable classes. **Benchmark/threshold:** the pre-pass should hit >99% catch on the planted classical six and CEC's enumerable gap classes (consistent with DeepPCB reference-method <0.2% error); if it does not, fix the diff/morphology before touching the VLM.
2. **Reframe the VLM seat as role 3 + narration only.** Drop absolute pour verdicts (role 1) and reference-comparison-as-judge (role 2) as VLM jobs — those become deterministic. If any comparison VLM is retained, frame it strictly **pairwise against the frozen reference** (CL-21.3), never absolute, and treat its output as a flag, never a verdict.
3. **Keep the safety envelope intact and fix the prompt.** VLM never measures, never gates; every VLM flag triggers a deterministic re-check (so a false positive costs one cheap re-check and a hallucination cannot affect a decision). Remove fed heuristics that predetermine answers (the "crossings > 0 ⇒ clipped" rule — mechanism 1 of the failure) and stop feeding numeric ground truth the model can parrot (mechanism 2; CL-21.1/21.2).
4. **Run the planted-defect battery with a render-only control.** Report per-class catch rate for the pre-pass, then the VLM's **incremental** catch rate on unknown-unknowns/toolchain bugs. **Decision threshold:** keep the VLM seat only if its incremental catch rate on the unknown-unknown battery is clearly > 0 *and* the latency/VRAM cost is acceptable; otherwise retire it and rely on the deterministic stack plus periodic human spot-checks. **A documented null result here is an acceptable outcome** — and the literature gives no reason to expect the VLM to beat deterministic diff on enumerable classes.

## Caveats
- **No PCB-specific VLM benchmark found.** Qwen3.6-35B-A3B served via llama.cpp was not individually benchmarked in any source; the failure-mode mapping is by analogy to the same documented mechanisms across many VLMs — the correct level of generalization for a charter, but not a board-specific measurement. (Documented absence.)
- **Task-domain transfer.** BlindTest/MMVP test abstract-shape and natural-image tasks, not PCB renders; the inference is that copper-pour counting/crossing/fragmentation detection sits in the same low-level-geometry family the literature shows VLMs fail. No paper tests PCB pour integrity by VLM directly. (Documented absence.)
- **BlindTest figure has two versions in the record:** 58.57% avg / 74.94% best (earlier) vs. 58.07% / 77.84% (latest arXiv revision). Cite the version used; the qualitative conclusion (~58% avg, far below human 100%) is stable.
- **The <0.2% reference-method error is DeepPCB-specific** under controlled imaging; CEC's perfect synthetic registration is at least as favorable, so it is a conservative lower bound — but CEC must still measure on its own planted battery rather than assume transfer.
- **Tier discipline.** Trade-press / practitioner / vendor sources (CAM350 and DFM blogs, LLM-as-judge vendor blogs, KiCad community forums) are **T4** corroboration only. Load-bearing claims rest on **T1** papers (BlindTest, MMVP, Words-or-Vision, POPE, MT-Bench, ViperGPT, Visual Programming, PatchCore, Tang/DeepPCB, Moganti) and **T3** primary docs (KiCad source/Doxygen for `DRCE_COPPER_SLIVER`; DownStream DFMStream / CAM350 product docs).
- **Searches that came back empty or thin (documented absences):** (a) direct VLM evaluation on PCB copper-pour or fill-integrity judgment — none found; (b) a head-to-head study quantifying incremental catch rate of a VLM *on top of* deterministic AOI — none found; existing VLM-AOI work (VELM, AnomalyGPT, ID-RAG) demonstrates classification/narration value, explicitly noting general-purpose VLMs "often fail at industrial surface anomaly detection due to hallucinations and imprecise localization," not detection value; (c) acid trap as a native KiCad DRC class — confirmed absent (must be a custom rule or external DFM tool).
