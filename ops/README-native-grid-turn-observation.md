# Native-grid turn observation

The broad sharp-turn inventory can amplify a one-nanometre coordinate mismatch
on a short trace into a false apparent deviation from a 45-degree turn. The
normal native craft, fabrication, current and pair gates remain unchanged.

`cec_native_quantized_turn_observation.observe` selects two actual same-net,
same-face, same-width native tracks with one unambiguous, coincident junction.
It derives their headings without a caller-supplied angular tolerance. An exact
rational witness must place the three common ideal vertices inside their closed
half-nanometre rounding cells and make both ideal strokes nonzero and exactly
octilinear, with exactly one 45-degree turn. A separate checker verifies every
equation, direction and bound. A hard90, backtrack, wrong net/layer/width, extra
branch or genuinely raw angle fails. Four rotations and both faces are tested.

This is only a local representation observation. It does NOT prove the source
file's literal endpoints are exactly octilinear, that the writer actually used
nearest rounding, that ideal and actual native copper polygons are equal, that
a whole route has one consistent ideal embedding, or that the route is needed,
electrically qualified or ready for manufacture. No source endpoint moves and
no acceptance threshold changes. The dashboard disposition is therefore
`positive_native_grid_45_turn`, never a fabricated physical repair or material
cover certificate. Unknown observations remain unknown.

The native-grid-aware review driver retains the original ordinary and unequal
power material observers. It tries this separate certificate only when the
material observer explicitly reports that the source is not a literal right
angle. It does not bypass other observer failures or any saved-board receiver.
