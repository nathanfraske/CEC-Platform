# Complete supply-kernel reembedding

This bounded owner handles a thin, causally connected routing kernel that
crosses ordinary routing vias. It is not an ordinary-signal width waiver or
an extension of the unchanged-width finishing owner. No board identifier,
reference designator, net spelling (other than the excluded Ground role), or
absolute coordinate controls its construction policy.

Read-only discovery follows native connectivity through under-floor outer
tracks and ordinary through vias. Full-current copper, component pads,
grouped barrels and even **ungrouped component-attached vias** terminate the
kernel. A via-in-pad remains a component interface, never disposable routing
debt. Discovery does not authorize deletion.

Planning rederives the unchanged positive current budget and actual-layer
fabrication floor. A transaction may retire at most 16 plain under-floor
tracks, four ordinary routing vias, and 32 mm of routing. It cannot retire
already current-sized tracks, change a part or anchor, or infer parallel
current sharing. The replacement must be strictly shorter, canonical 0/45
geometry, sized for the entire existing current budget on every stroke, and
join different surviving native components through two retained through-via
centres. Unknown current, Kelvin, coupled-pair and Ground work is refused.

Saved receiving independently reconstructs source-minus-exact-cut-plus-path,
including Ground-only refill. All old surviving native components, drilled
terminal connections, pre-existing full-width planar joins, parts, groups,
lands, sidecars and current requirements are preserved. The original whole
current-debt checker is rerun. New planar or plated capacity is **not**
credited. Native custom-rule DRC, field coupling, clipped Kelvin, all known
Ground returns, pair/reference sheets, labels, dimensions, stack and release
checks remain separate mandatory obligations. This module grants no barrel,
thermal, electrical, fabrication or production qualification.

The integration case exposed why complete native receiving matters: deleting
the entire thin Hub pickup still left a genuine pour-to-pour open even while
the selected source/sink current checker reported unchanged debt. A replacement
between existing barrels restored that connection without moving power fills.
The general repair is to reconstruct the complete causal kernel, not repeatedly
delete two visible corner strokes or assume that filled power regions connect.

Synthetic tests use opaque names, all four rotations, both outer faces, actual
native pad/barrel geometry, ungrouped via-in-pad roots, and saved mutation
refusals. Their declared loads are fixture-only, not new hardware budgets.
