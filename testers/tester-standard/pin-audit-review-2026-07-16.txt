## ADDENDUM 2 (orchestrator safety check-in, before 03/06 close): TWO more
## parts added, MM74HC273WM (LCSC C906662, octal D-FF w/ async CLEAR,
## SOIC-20) and CD4051BM96 (LCSC C21379, 8-channel analog mux, SOIC-16).
## REASON: the orchestrator's safety review of the shift-register-expander
## plan (addendum 1, above) surfaced two real gaps, both now fixed in the
## design (see 03-mcu.kicad_sch / 06a-06c's own notes for the as-drawn
## detail):
## (1) TRIP TIMESTAMPING: a trip signal that ONLY lives behind the polled
##     74HC165 chain degrades a promised us-class stamp to poll cadence.
##     Fix: all 12 trip-comparator outputs (4 loop + 5 bank + 3 SCP) feed a
##     diode-OR "TRIP_ANY" node (pull-down default) landing on a DIRECT MCU
##     GPIO interrupt AND the shared CLOCK input of 2x MM74HC273 (12 of 16
##     D-inputs used, one per trip signal, D tied logic-high so the trip
##     line's own rising edge self-clocks a permanent capture into Q,
##     independent of what the source signal does afterward) -- the Q
##     outputs then feed the 74HC165 poll chain for per-leg detail,
##     "un-decayed" between polls. All 12+ latches share ONE async CLEAR
##     line, which IS an expander output bit (matches the orchestrator's own
##     allowed list: "arm/reset/test-fire" already named "reset" as an OK
##     expander duty).
## (2) PROTECTION PATHS NEVER TRANSIT THE EXPANDER:
##     (a) SCP crowbar FIRE: the shared "fire" pulse is now its OWN DIRECT
##         MCU GPIO (not an expander bit), ANDed per-block (SN74AHCT1G08,
##         already platform-vendored, reused here as a genuine 2-input AND
##         rather than its Hub-Standard buffer role) with that block's own
##         ARM state (an expander bit -- arming stays slow/deliberate and
##         CAN live on the expander per the orchestrator's allowed list).
##         Neither signal alone can fire a crowbar; the expander is never
##         the sole gate on a hard-short action.
##     (b) De-gate dead-man rail: driven by its OWN direct MCU GPIO (not
##         multiplexed) -- the DEGATE_RAIL net still reaches every hot-side
##         gate driver as a project-wide `global_label` (a SCHEMATIC fan-out
##         mechanism only; electrically it is the ONE direct GPIO).
##     (c) Shift-chain OE#: a direct MCU GPIO, PULLED UP (not driven) by
##         default -- 74HC595 OE# is active-LOW, so an un-driven/reset MCU
##         leaves OE# HIGH = every '595 output Hi-Z = every hot-side gate
##         pull-down (already local per-cluster, split-architecture rule)
##         reads its default OFF state. Firmware only pulls OE# low after
##         POST has verified sane bank/SCP state. This is the "standard fix"
##         the orchestrator named; it also functionally reinforces (b), the
##         dead-man rail.
## GPIO-BUDGET REBALANCE (this forced a second pass on the 03-mcu map from
## addendum 1): the 6 NTC + DETECT_SENSE analog channels (7, previously 7
## DIRECT ADC pins) now route through the CD4051 mux -> ONE ADC pin, with
## the 3 select lines on the expander (non-critical, slow-changing) --
## netting SIX GPIOs back, which fully covers the four new direct-GPIO
## safety additions (TRIP_ANY, SCP_FIRE_SHARED, DEGATE_RAIL drive,
## SHIFT_OE#) with margin to spare. Final ESP32-C6-MINI-1-N4 map: 17 of 20
## GPIOs used, 3 spare -- see 03-mcu.kicad_sch's own caption for the full
## pin table.

## ADDENDUM 1 (03-mcu capture): 74HC165D,653 (LCSC C5613, Nexperia) added.
## REASON -- a real GPIO-budget problem found during 03-mcu capture: the
## ESP32-C6-MINI-1-N4 exposes exactly 20 usable GPIOs, but the ST board's
## raw signal count (4 PWM setpoints, ~31 bank/SCP/display control bits,
## ~16 trip/tach/button inputs, DETECT + 6 NTC analog channels, CAN, USB,
## BOOT) is roughly 3x that. RESOLUTION: a shared shift-register expansion
## bus -- 4x 74HC595 (cascaded, 32 output bits: bank-group CTRL, SCP arm/
## fire, LCD CS x7, backlight enable) + 2x 74HC165 (cascaded, 16 input
## bits: loop/bank/SCP trip, fan tach, service button) -- on a 3-pin shared
## half-duplex bus (SCK/DATA/STROBE; DATA reverses direction between the
## shift-out and shift-in phases, and STROBE double-duty's as both the '595
## chain's RCLK latch and the '165 chain's SH/LD parallel-load strobe).
## This is NOT an invented workaround: docs/psu-tester-architecture-sketch-
## 2026-07-16.md Sec5 (displays) explicitly anticipates it for backlight
## PWM ("fits the ST tier's C6 pin budget via the same expander"); this
## capture generalizes the SAME expander to bank/SCP/loop-trip duty, which
## the source docs did not spell out but the pin budget makes necessary.
## Full bit allocation + the resulting 03-mcu GPIO map (final: 19 of 20
## GPIOs used, 1 spare) are documented in 03-mcu.kicad_sch's own caption/
## note and in FOLLOWUPS.md. Two simplifications this forced, both flagged
## as deliberate, not silent: (1) pin 7 (SYNC/FREEZE at ENT tier) stays a
## platform-locked "reserved spare" at consumer/Standard tier (Sec2.3) --
## its mis-plug PROTECTION chain (series R + SMAJ58A) is still populated
## on 01-link per the owner's PoE-safe directive, but it is NOT wired to an
## MCU GPIO, since ST is not ENT; (2) the 5VSB-peak "loop" (04d) has no
## continuous PWM+RC/op-amp CC cell -- its 3.5A/500ms peak leg is a spec-
## compliance PULSE test (fixed duration burst), not a swept analog
## measurement, so it is modeled as a shift-out-driven ON/OFF switch (still
## carries its own Kelvin shunt + trip-watch cell, matching every other
## loop's measurement architecture) rather than a 4th analog setpoint
## channel it does not need.

## Tooling bug found + fixed during promotion (not a part issue): the FIRST
## promotion attempt failed `kicad-cli sym/fp upgrade` with an opaque
## "Unable to load library" on AOD4184A specifically. Root-caused by
## isolating single symbols and diffing a kicad-cli-upgraded copy against a
## raw extraction: (1) a `descr`/`ki_description` string containing a
## LITERAL embedded newline (a multi-line datasheet-link blurb easyeda2kicad
## emitted for this part) breaks kicad-cli's loader for the older/compact
## easyeda2kicad symbol format, even though the parens balance and a real
## from-scratch parser (cec_sym_audit.py) reads it fine -- fixed by
## flattening any multi-line string literal to one line before upgrading
## (cosmetic-only field); (2) re-labeling an un-upgraded symbol body under a
## modern `(version 20251024)` header (this agent's own first-draft
## approach) makes kicad-cli parse it via the strict modern path, which the
## older compact body does not satisfy (missing the per-symbol
## `(embedded_fonts no)` child real KiCad-10 output always carries) -- fixed
## by running `kicad-cli sym upgrade` on each SOURCE file individually
## BEFORE extracting/concatenating, so every carved block is already fully
## modern and self-contained. Both fixes are generic (in the promotion
## script, not part-specific); every part in this library was re-verified to
## load cleanly via `kicad-cli sym/fp upgrade --force` after the fix.

## Addendum 4 (Phase B, 01-link + 02-power verification pass): a genuine
## HARDWARE-CORRECTNESS bug found and fixed, plus a batch of layout/ERC
## debugging notes worth keeping for the remaining leaves.
##
## SAFETY-RELEVANT FIND: both diode-OR circuits (01-link's D3/SS34, ORing
## the tester's local +5V_LOGIC into the RJ-45/mezzanine mis-plug chain's
## +5V_PROT rail; 02-power's D6/SS34, ORing the 12V aux barrel jack into
## buck1's +12V_FAN output) were wired BACKWARDS on the first capture pass
## -- anode on the SHARED/downstream rail, cathode on the DEDICATED source,
## the opposite of correct diode-OR polarity (anode faces its own source,
## cathode faces the rail it feeds into so the rail can never push current
## back out through the diode into that source). Root cause: the real
## `cec-vendor:D_Schottky` symbol is pin1=K(cathode)/pin2=A(anode) --
## VERIFIED directly against the symbol body (D_Schottky_1_1: pin number
## "1" name "K", pin number "2" name "A", and the graphical triangle/cathode
## -bar glyph agrees) -- but the code that first wired both diodes assumed
## the opposite (variable names `a3`/`k3` had pin1=A), an assumption never
## checked against the actual symbol text. Caught in stages: (1) 02-power's
## D6 first showed as ERC `power_pin_not_driven` on U7.VIN (+12V_FAN looked
## undriven) -- tracing +12V_FAN's real drivers led to the diode and its
## pin identities; (2) fixing D6's polarity by simply swapping which net
## table entry got which pin number, without changing placement, exposed a
## SECOND bug on 01-link's D3 once the same fix was applied there: ERC
## `multiple_net_names` fired ("+5V_PROT and +5V_LOGIC attached to the same
## items") because the corrected wire, run to the pin that is now farther
## from the junction point, passed geometrically THROUGH the other (nearer)
## pin's own coordinate along the way -- KiCad binds a pin wherever a drawn
## wire's path touches it, not only at declared endpoints, so an unlucky
## collinear routing silently shorted the two nets back together. Fixed by
## rotating each diode 180 degrees from its original placement (270 instead
## of 90 for D3; 180 instead of 0 for D6) so the pin that must sit NEAR the
## junction (to avoid pass-through) is also the pin that must be there
## electrically (cathode-to-rail for D3, anode-to-jack for D6) -- verified
## by direct `Compose.pin()` coordinate probes before and after, and by the
## exported netlist (`kicad-cli sch export netlist`) showing +5V_PROT/
## +5V_LOGIC and +12V_FAN/+12V_AUX_RAW as fully separate nets with the
## correct pin on each side. LESSON for the remaining leaves (04-loops'
## trip-watch diodes, 06-scp's crowbar diodes, any other D_Schottky/SS34/
## SS110/SMAJ58A instance): always query real pin identity from the symbol
## body (or an empirical `c.pin()` probe cross-checked against the symbol
## text) before wiring a diode-OR or series-block role, and after wiring,
## check that neither wire endpoint sits collinear-and-passing-through a
## third pin on the same net axis -- both bugs here were caught only by
## ERC + netlist re-verification after the fact, not by inspection.
##

## `power_in`. Per the real TI datasheet (lib/datasheets/TPS54331DR_C9865.

## power MOSFET"), PH is an ACTIVELY DRIVEN node, never an external power

##
## MECHANICAL BUG CLASS (three occurrences, all fixed the same way):
## `c.use((ref, pin))` marks a pin CONSUMED (skip the generic per-net auto-
## stub+label pass) but does not itself draw anything -- if nothing else in
## the leaf's hand-wiring actually reaches that exact pin, it is left a
## bare, label-less, wire-less connection point. Found on: the buck-stage
## EN pin (marked used, never wired -- ERC `pin_not_connected`+
## `pin_not_driven`), the feedback divider's rail-side tap pin (R17/R19
## pin1, same symptom), and (a variant of the same class) D6's J3/C15 tap
## point being fully hand-consumed with no explicit label, which is
## electrically fine but left the net exported-netlist-visible only under
## KiCad's own auto name ("Net-(D6-A)") instead of "+12V_AUX_RAW". Fix
## pattern used throughout: either leave the pin genuinely UNCONSUMED so
## the generic pass auto-labels it (preferred -- matches this leaf's own
## stated design philosophy of hand-wiring less), or if it must be hand-
## consumed, always pair the `c.use()` with an explicit `c.wire()`/
## `c.label()` reaching that literal pin coordinate.
##
## LAYOUT/READABILITY (cec_sch_layout --check-overlaps, --check-wires):
## both leaves reached 0 overlapping text pairs. Two reusable findings for
## the remaining leaves: (1) a USB-C receptacle symbol (and likely similar
## multi-redundant-pin connectors) draws its mirrored-orientation duplicate
## pins (VBUS A4/A9/B4/B9; GND A1/A12/B1/B12) fully COINCIDENT at one point
## each -- leaving all of them unconsumed makes the generic pass stamp one
## full label/power-flag glyph per member on top of each other (self-
## collisions, and on --check-wires, glyph-clip). Consuming all-but-one of
## a coincident group with NO wire is safe (KiCad joins same-point pins
## exactly like a drawn wire), and is NOT the "marked used but never wired"
## bug above, because there IS a real connection there already (the shared
## point). (2) At tight IC pin pitch (SOIC-8 etc.), placing a small
## unrelated part's own unconsumed net member near a pin cluster it isn't
## electrically part of (e.g. a bootstrap cap sitting near the VIN/EN/SS
## side instead of near the BOOT/PH side it actually bridges) both reads
## wrong AND collides -- moving the part to sit near the pins it actually
## connects to fixed both problems at once. `cec_sch_layout.py --nudge`
## only repositions FIELD text (Reference/Value), never net labels -- it is
## NOT idempotent across a `gen_tester_st.py` regeneration (regeneration
## rewrites the file from source, discarding any nudge), so the working
## order for every future leaf is: generate -> --nudge every leaf touched
## -> --check-overlaps every leaf touched, repeated until 0, immediately
## before ERC/netlist/commit.
