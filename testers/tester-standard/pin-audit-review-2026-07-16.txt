## ADDENDUM 2 (orchestrator safety check-in, before 03/06 close): TWO more
## parts added, MM74HC273WM (LCSC C906662, octal D-FF w/ async CLEAR,
## SOIC-20) and CD4051BM96 (LCSC C21379, 8-channel analog mux, SOIC-16).
## REASON: the orchestrator's safety review of the shift-register-expander
## plan (addendum 1, above) surfaced two real gaps, both now fixed in the
## design (see 03-mcu.kicad_sch / 06a-06c's own notes for the as-drawn
## detail):
## (1) TRIP TIMESTAMPING: a trip signal that ONLY lives behind the polled
##     74HC165 chain degrades a promised us-class stamp to poll cadence.
##     Fix: all 12 trip-comparator outputs (4 loop + 5 bank + 3 SCP) feed a
##     diode-OR "TRIP_ANY" node (pull-down default) landing on a DIRECT MCU
##     GPIO interrupt AND the shared CLOCK input of 2x MM74HC273 (12 of 16
##     D-inputs used, one per trip signal, D tied logic-high so the trip
##     line's own rising edge self-clocks a permanent capture into Q,
##     independent of what the source signal does afterward) -- the Q
##     outputs then feed the 74HC165 poll chain for per-leg detail,
##     "un-decayed" between polls. All 12+ latches share ONE async CLEAR
##     line, which IS an expander output bit (matches the orchestrator's own
##     allowed list: "arm/reset/test-fire" already named "reset" as an OK
##     expander duty).
## (2) PROTECTION PATHS NEVER TRANSIT THE EXPANDER:
##     (a) SCP crowbar FIRE: the shared "fire" pulse is now its OWN DIRECT
##         MCU GPIO (not an expander bit), ANDed per-block (SN74AHCT1G08,
##         already platform-vendored, reused here as a genuine 2-input AND
##         rather than its Hub-Standard buffer role) with that block's own
##         ARM state (an expander bit -- arming stays slow/deliberate and
##         CAN live on the expander per the orchestrator's allowed list).
##         Neither signal alone can fire a crowbar; the expander is never
##         the sole gate on a hard-short action.
##     (b) De-gate dead-man rail: driven by its OWN direct MCU GPIO (not
##         multiplexed) -- the DEGATE_RAIL net still reaches every hot-side
##         gate driver as a project-wide `global_label` (a SCHEMATIC fan-out
##         mechanism only; electrically it is the ONE direct GPIO).
##     (c) Shift-chain OE#: a direct MCU GPIO, PULLED UP (not driven) by
##         default -- 74HC595 OE# is active-LOW, so an un-driven/reset MCU
##         leaves OE# HIGH = every '595 output Hi-Z = every hot-side gate
##         pull-down (already local per-cluster, split-architecture rule)
##         reads its default OFF state. Firmware only pulls OE# low after
##         POST has verified sane bank/SCP state. This is the "standard fix"
##         the orchestrator named; it also functionally reinforces (b), the
##         dead-man rail.
## GPIO-BUDGET REBALANCE (this forced a second pass on the 03-mcu map from
## addendum 1): the 6 NTC + DETECT_SENSE analog channels (7, previously 7
## DIRECT ADC pins) now route through the CD4051 mux -> ONE ADC pin, with
## the 3 select lines on the expander (non-critical, slow-changing) --
## netting SIX GPIOs back, which fully covers the four new direct-GPIO
## safety additions (TRIP_ANY, SCP_FIRE_SHARED, DEGATE_RAIL drive,
## SHIFT_OE#) with margin to spare. Final ESP32-C6-MINI-1-N4 map: 17 of 20
## GPIOs used, 3 spare -- see 03-mcu.kicad_sch's own caption for the full
## pin table.

## ADDENDUM 1 (03-mcu capture): 74HC165D,653 (LCSC C5613, Nexperia) added.
## REASON -- a real GPIO-budget problem found during 03-mcu capture: the
## ESP32-C6-MINI-1-N4 exposes exactly 20 usable GPIOs, but the ST board's
## raw signal count (4 PWM setpoints, ~31 bank/SCP/display control bits,
## ~16 trip/tach/button inputs, DETECT + 6 NTC analog channels, CAN, USB,
## BOOT) is roughly 3x that. RESOLUTION: a shared shift-register expansion
## bus -- 4x 74HC595 (cascaded, 32 output bits: bank-group CTRL, SCP arm/
## fire, LCD CS x7, backlight enable) + 2x 74HC165 (cascaded, 16 input
## bits: loop/bank/SCP trip, fan tach, service button) -- on a 3-pin shared
## half-duplex bus (SCK/DATA/STROBE; DATA reverses direction between the
## shift-out and shift-in phases, and STROBE double-duty's as both the '595
## chain's RCLK latch and the '165 chain's SH/LD parallel-load strobe).
## This is NOT an invented workaround: docs/psu-tester-architecture-sketch-
## 2026-07-16.md Sec5 (displays) explicitly anticipates it for backlight
## PWM ("fits the ST tier's C6 pin budget via the same expander"); this
## capture generalizes the SAME expander to bank/SCP/loop-trip duty, which
## the source docs did not spell out but the pin budget makes necessary.
## Full bit allocation + the resulting 03-mcu GPIO map (final: 19 of 20
## GPIOs used, 1 spare) are documented in 03-mcu.kicad_sch's own caption/
## note and in FOLLOWUPS.md. Two simplifications this forced, both flagged
## as deliberate, not silent: (1) pin 7 (SYNC/FREEZE at ENT tier) stays a
## platform-locked "reserved spare" at consumer/Standard tier (Sec2.3) --
## its mis-plug PROTECTION chain (series R + SMAJ58A) is still populated
## on 01-link per the owner's PoE-safe directive, but it is NOT wired to an
## MCU GPIO, since ST is not ENT; (2) the 5VSB-peak "loop" (04d) has no
## continuous PWM+RC/op-amp CC cell -- its 3.5A/500ms peak leg is a spec-
## compliance PULSE test (fixed duration burst), not a swept analog
## measurement, so it is modeled as a shift-out-driven ON/OFF switch (still
## carries its own Kelvin shunt + trip-watch cell, matching every other
## loop's measurement architecture) rather than a 4th analog setpoint
## channel it does not need.

## Tooling bug found + fixed during promotion (not a part issue): the FIRST
## promotion attempt failed `kicad-cli sym/fp upgrade` with an opaque
## "Unable to load library" on AOD4184A specifically. Root-caused by
## isolating single symbols and diffing a kicad-cli-upgraded copy against a
## raw extraction: (1) a `descr`/`ki_description` string containing a
## LITERAL embedded newline (a multi-line datasheet-link blurb easyeda2kicad
## emitted for this part) breaks kicad-cli's loader for the older/compact
## easyeda2kicad symbol format, even though the parens balance and a real
## from-scratch parser (cec_sym_audit.py) reads it fine -- fixed by
## flattening any multi-line string literal to one line before upgrading
## (cosmetic-only field); (2) re-labeling an un-upgraded symbol body under a
## modern `(version 20251024)` header (this agent's own first-draft
## approach) makes kicad-cli parse it via the strict modern path, which the
## older compact body does not satisfy (missing the per-symbol
## `(embedded_fonts no)` child real KiCad-10 output always carries) -- fixed
## by running `kicad-cli sym upgrade` on each SOURCE file individually
## BEFORE extracting/concatenating, so every carved block is already fully
## modern and self-contained. Both fixes are generic (in the promotion
## script, not part-specific); every part in this library was re-verified to
## load cleanly via `kicad-cli sym/fp upgrade --force` after the fix.

## Addendum 4 (Phase B, 01-link + 02-power verification pass): a genuine
## HARDWARE-CORRECTNESS bug found and fixed, plus a batch of layout/ERC
## debugging notes worth keeping for the remaining leaves.
##
## SAFETY-RELEVANT FIND: both diode-OR circuits (01-link's D3/SS34, ORing
## the tester's local +5V_LOGIC into the RJ-45/mezzanine mis-plug chain's
## +5V_PROT rail; 02-power's D6/SS34, ORing the 12V aux barrel jack into
## buck1's +12V_FAN output) were wired BACKWARDS on the first capture pass
## -- anode on the SHARED/downstream rail, cathode on the DEDICATED source,
## the opposite of correct diode-OR polarity (anode faces its own source,
## cathode faces the rail it feeds into so the rail can never push current
## back out through the diode into that source). Root cause: the real
## `cec-vendor:D_Schottky` symbol is pin1=K(cathode)/pin2=A(anode) --
## VERIFIED directly against the symbol body (D_Schottky_1_1: pin number
## "1" name "K", pin number "2" name "A", and the graphical triangle/cathode
## -bar glyph agrees) -- but the code that first wired both diodes assumed
## the opposite (variable names `a3`/`k3` had pin1=A), an assumption never
## checked against the actual symbol text. Caught in stages: (1) 02-power's
## D6 first showed as ERC `power_pin_not_driven` on U7.VIN (+12V_FAN looked
## undriven) -- tracing +12V_FAN's real drivers led to the diode and its
## pin identities; (2) fixing D6's polarity by simply swapping which net
## table entry got which pin number, without changing placement, exposed a
## SECOND bug on 01-link's D3 once the same fix was applied there: ERC
## `multiple_net_names` fired ("+5V_PROT and +5V_LOGIC attached to the same
## items") because the corrected wire, run to the pin that is now farther
## from the junction point, passed geometrically THROUGH the other (nearer)
## pin's own coordinate along the way -- KiCad binds a pin wherever a drawn
## wire's path touches it, not only at declared endpoints, so an unlucky
## collinear routing silently shorted the two nets back together. Fixed by
## rotating each diode 180 degrees from its original placement (270 instead
## of 90 for D3; 180 instead of 0 for D6) so the pin that must sit NEAR the
## junction (to avoid pass-through) is also the pin that must be there
## electrically (cathode-to-rail for D3, anode-to-jack for D6) -- verified
## by direct `Compose.pin()` coordinate probes before and after, and by the
## exported netlist (`kicad-cli sch export netlist`) showing +5V_PROT/
## +5V_LOGIC and +12V_FAN/+12V_AUX_RAW as fully separate nets with the
## correct pin on each side. LESSON for the remaining leaves (04-loops'
## trip-watch diodes, 06-scp's crowbar diodes, any other D_Schottky/SS34/
## SS110/SMAJ58A instance): always query real pin identity from the symbol
## body (or an empirical `c.pin()` probe cross-checked against the symbol
## text) before wiring a diode-OR or series-block role, and after wiring,
## check that neither wire endpoint sits collinear-and-passing-through a
## third pin on the same net axis -- both bugs here were caught only by
## ERC + netlist re-verification after the fact, not by inspection.
##
## SEPARATE PIN-TYPE FIX: cec-tester:TPS54331DR's pin 8 (PH, the internal
## high-side MOSFET's switching node) was pulled from easyeda2kicad typed
## `power_in`. Per the real TI datasheet (lib/datasheets/TPS54331DR_C9865.
## pdf, pin description table: "PH 8 The source of the internal high-side
## power MOSFET"), PH is an ACTIVELY DRIVEN node, never an external power
## input -- retyped to `power_out` in lib/cec-tester.kicad_sym (both U6 and
## U7 share the one symbol definition). Re-ran `cec_sym_audit.py --audit
## lib/cec-tester.kicad_sym` after the edit: still high=0 medium=7 low=86,
## PH does not appear in either list (accepted at every severity).
##
## MECHANICAL BUG CLASS (three occurrences, all fixed the same way):
## `c.use((ref, pin))` marks a pin CONSUMED (skip the generic per-net auto-
## stub+label pass) but does not itself draw anything -- if nothing else in
## the leaf's hand-wiring actually reaches that exact pin, it is left a
## bare, label-less, wire-less connection point. Found on: the buck-stage
## EN pin (marked used, never wired -- ERC `pin_not_connected`+
## `pin_not_driven`), the feedback divider's rail-side tap pin (R17/R19
## pin1, same symptom), and (a variant of the same class) D6's J3/C15 tap
## point being fully hand-consumed with no explicit label, which is
## electrically fine but left the net exported-netlist-visible only under
## KiCad's own auto name ("Net-(D6-A)") instead of "+12V_AUX_RAW". Fix
## pattern used throughout: either leave the pin genuinely UNCONSUMED so
## the generic pass auto-labels it (preferred -- matches this leaf's own
## stated design philosophy of hand-wiring less), or if it must be hand-
## consumed, always pair the `c.use()` with an explicit `c.wire()`/
## `c.label()` reaching that literal pin coordinate.
##
## LAYOUT/READABILITY (cec_sch_layout --check-overlaps, --check-wires):
## both leaves reached 0 overlapping text pairs. Two reusable findings for
## the remaining leaves: (1) a USB-C receptacle symbol (and likely similar
## multi-redundant-pin connectors) draws its mirrored-orientation duplicate
## pins (VBUS A4/A9/B4/B9; GND A1/A12/B1/B12) fully COINCIDENT at one point
## each -- leaving all of them unconsumed makes the generic pass stamp one
## full label/power-flag glyph per member on top of each other (self-
## collisions, and on --check-wires, glyph-clip). Consuming all-but-one of
## a coincident group with NO wire is safe (KiCad joins same-point pins
## exactly like a drawn wire), and is NOT the "marked used but never wired"
## bug above, because there IS a real connection there already (the shared
## point). (2) At tight IC pin pitch (SOIC-8 etc.), placing a small
## unrelated part's own unconsumed net member near a pin cluster it isn't
## electrically part of (e.g. a bootstrap cap sitting near the VIN/EN/SS
## side instead of near the BOOT/PH side it actually bridges) both reads
## wrong AND collides -- moving the part to sit near the pins it actually
## connects to fixed both problems at once. `cec_sch_layout.py --nudge`
## only repositions FIELD text (Reference/Value), never net labels -- it is
## NOT idempotent across a `gen_tester_st.py` regeneration (regeneration
## rewrites the file from source, discarding any nudge), so the working
## order for every future leaf is: generate -> --nudge every leaf touched
## -> --check-overlaps every leaf touched, repeated until 0, immediately
## before ERC/netlist/commit.

## (4x 74HC595 / 2x 74HC165 / 2x MM74HC273 trip latches / CD4051 mux). Five
## real design/wiring bugs were found and fixed DURING capture (none of
## these were present in the pre-capture addenda 1/2 design -- they are
## CAPTURE mistakes, caught by the same generate -> ERC/netlist/overlap
## verify loop, not new design decisions):
##
## (1) U8's GND pin collapsed to ONE pin number. `name_to_number()` (the
## symbol pin-name->number lookup shared with gen_p4_t1_block.py's own P4
## map) keeps only the LAST match per name -- fine for every single-
## instance signal, WRONG for GND: ESP32-C6-MINI-1-N4 exposes 22 separate
## GND pins/pads (1,2,11,14 + the 36-53 exposed-pad cluster), and only pin
## 53 was ever actually wired; the other 21 were floating, silently, with
## no ERC complaint (a genuinely disconnected power pin with no net table
## entry at all doesn't trip `pin_not_connected` the way a mis-routed
## SIGNAL pin does). Caught by re-deriving the exported netlist's own net
## list and finding 20 `unconnected-(U8-GND-PadN)` entries. Fixed with a
## new `all_pins_named()` helper (returns every match, not just the last)
## and `C6_GND_PINS`, used for the GND net table instead of `C6["GND"]`.
## FOLLOWUPS.md flags this as a class of bug to check on U8's +3V3 uses
## too if a future MCU variant ever exposes multiple 3V3 pins (this one
## doesn't -- verified, only pin 3).
##
## (2) EN and BOOT pullups wired BACKWARDS -- the more serious sibling of
## Addendum 4's diode-polarity bug, same root cause (assuming a pin
## relationship instead of checking what was actually drawn). The net
## table correctly declared R40/R41 pin1 as the +3V3 side and pin2 as the
## MCU-signal side, but the WIRING code connected U8.EN/IO9 to pin1 (the
## side the table said was +3V3) and marked pin1 "consumed" -- so no
## auto-flag ever fired there, and the pullup's real copper had NO path to
## +3V3 at all: EN's actual built circuit was just "SW3-to-GND + C42",
## permanently missing the pull-up half, i.e. EN would float low-ish
## whenever the button wasn't pressed rather than defaulting HIGH
## (enabled). Caught by re-deriving the exported +3V3 net's membership
## (43 expected components, U8/C40/R40/R41 all silently absent) after the
## `power_pin_not_driven` puzzle below pointed at "something about this
## leaf's +3V3 net is incomplete." Fixed by re-wiring the MCU pin to pin2
## instead, leaving pin1 genuinely unconsumed for the auto-flag -- matches
## the file's own established "wire to the near pin, leave the far pin for
## the generic pass" pattern from the 02-power buck-stage fix.
##
## (3) `power_pin_not_driven` on exactly one +3V3 instance and one GND
## instance (out of 47 and 77 same-named members respectively) -- a real
## puzzle, root-caused by elimination (coordinate archaeology: reverse-
## computed both flagged points' grid position via the leaf's placement-
## to-real-mm offset, checked for wire pass-through, duplicate coordinates,
## and UUID collisions -- all clean) before landing on the right
## explanation: this leaf, unlike 01-link and 02-power, has NO local
## regulator for EITHER rail -- +3V3 is entirely 01-link's LP5907 output
## and GND is entirely the RJ-45 jack shell, both arriving purely via
## cross-sheet global-power-symbol name-merging that does not exist yet in
## a standalone-leaf test. ERC reports exactly ONE representative
## violation per undriven NET (not "these 2 pins are special"), and with
## every OTHER rail on this leaf already needing a `powerflag_nets` entry
## (+12V_FAN), it was easy to miss that +3V3/GND ALSO qualified here even
## though they didn't on the other two leaves (where +3V3 IS locally
## regulated). Fixed: `+3V3` and `GND` added to `L03.powerflag_nets`.
##
## (4) MM74HC273 D-inputs first captured tied HIGH. This was a REAL
## conceptual error, not just a wiring slip: with every D pinned to +3V3
## and CLOCK=TRIP_ANY shared across all 12 real bits, EVERY Q would latch
## HIGH identically on ANY single trip -- there would be no way to tell
## WHICH rail/loop/SCP block actually tripped from the latch outputs,
## defeating the entire "per-leg detail read via comparator-side LATCHES"
## design the orchestrator's own safety review (addendum 2) asked for.
## Fixed: each of the 12 real trip sources (the same `_trip_detail_names`
## the 165 chain already used) now drives its own D-input (global_net, the
## leaf that owns each comparator will tap in when built); each Q -- the
## LATCHED snapshot -- feeds the matching 74HC165 D-input over a new LOCAL
## net (`{name}_LATCH`); the 4 unused D/Q pairs (16 hardware bits, 12 real
## sources) have their D tied GND (never leave a digital input floating)
## and their Q left in no net at all (an unused OUTPUT driving nothing
## needs no tie, unlike an input).
##
## (5) MM74HC273 CLEAR pins entirely unwired, AND no expander bit was
## budgeted for them. Both chips' CLEAR (pin 1) showed as
## `unconnected-(U15/16-CLEAR-Pad1)` in the netlist -- the addenda's own
## design said the shared async CLEAR "IS an expander output bit" but the
## actual 32-bit 595 budget built in this capture (19 bank + 3 SCP-arm + 7
## LCD-CS + 3 mux-select) left no room for it. Root cause: the budget was
## planned before capture without re-checking against what got literally
## implemented. Fixed by trimming ONE bit, not by discovering new headroom
## -- LCD_CS_MAIN is not really an expander duty at all: the main display
## sits on its own header with no other device sharing its bus, so it
## needs no chip-select signal whatsoever (07-displays will hardwire the
## pin active). That freed exactly the one CLEAR_SHARED bit needed, at the
## cost of 07-displays inheriting a one-line hardwiring note instead of a
## generated bit.
##
## (6, smaller) `SHIFT_MR_INACTIVE` -- same class as (2): declared as its
## OWN isolated 4-member net instead of actually being added to +3V3, so
## all four 595 ~{MR} (active-low master reset) pins were tied together
## but floating -- a real noise-susceptibility risk on a reset input, not
## just cosmetic. Fixed by folding those 4 pins into the +3V3 net table
## instead of giving them a separate net name.
##
## LAYOUT: reached 0 cec_sch_layout --check-overlaps via the same
## established techniques (widen tight IC-to-IC spacing so independently-
## auto-labeled, real-pin-pitch-spaced signals stop landing on each other;
## move a long note() block clear of whatever now occupies its old
## position after other placement changed) plus ONE new one: this leaf's
## total content is tall enough that A2 paper put a bottom-of-sheet note()
## and a stray power-flag PAST the page's own printable frame (`cec_sch_
## lint`'s SL-09) -- fixed by moving the leaf to A1, not by fighting exact
## coordinates. `cec_sch_layout.py --check-overlaps` does not itself check
## the page-frame boundary; `cec_sch_lint.py` (SL-09) is the one that
## catches it, so both need to be green, not just the overlap count.
##
## GPIO / bit-budget final state (all real, capture-time findings folded
## in): 20 of 20 direct GPIOs committed (zero spare -- flagged in
## FOLLOWUPS.md), 32 of 32 595 output bits committed (zero spare), 16 of

## REAL BUG #1 (SAFETY-RELEVANT, library-level): cec-tester:OPA2277UA's
## channel-B sub-symbol (OPA2277UA_2_1) was drawn at THE EXACT SAME local
## pin coordinates as channel A's (INB+/INB-/OUTB numerically coincide with
## INA+/INA-/OUTA) -- standard, CORRECT KiCad practice for a multi-unit part
## (only one unit is ever shown/placed at a time in real KiCad), but THIS
## project's own cec_sch.py pin_table() scanner flattens ALL units' pins
## into ONE merged table applied to every placed instance regardless of the
## hardcoded `(unit 1)` (the same "engine limit" the Phase-A OPA2277
## decision already named) -- so leaving channel B "unwired/spare" as
## Phase-A intended does NOT leave it floating: OUTB (an independent,
## still-powered internal amplifier output, since V+/V- are shared pins)
## lands exactly on OUTA, MY ACTIVELY-DRIVEN loop output. Verified harmless-
## but-real via direct coordinate probes (cec_sch_layout.pin_abs_rot) before
## concluding it needed a fix rather than a footnote: INB+/INB- also
## coincide with INA+/INA- (channel B would see the identical inputs, so
## the paralleled internal amplifier targets the identical output -- not a
## cross-rail short, but two live op-amp outputs wired together is bad
## practice regardless of matched targets, and ERC correctly flagged it as
## `no_connect_connected` once channel B's NC flags landed on top of
## channel A's real, connected pins). FIX (library, lib/cec-tester.kicad_sym):
## relocated the WHOLE OPA2277UA_2_1 sub-block (triangle glyph + both tick
## polylines + all 3 pins) by a rigid -19.05mm Y shift (15 grid units --
## the FIRST attempt used an arbitrary -20mm, which is NOT a 1.27mm grid
## multiple and tripped a NEW cec_sch_lint SL-01 "off-grid no_connect"
## ERROR on the 3 now-genuinely-isolated channel-B pins; caught by re-
## running the lint gate, not assumed clean) so channel B is now GENUINELY
## floating, matching the Phase-A note's actual intent. cec_sym_audit.py
## --audit unchanged (17 symbols, high=0 medium=7 low=89) and `kicad-cli
## sym upgrade` round-trips clean after the edit. Benefits every future
## OPA2277UA instance platform-wide, not just these 4 leaves.

## REAL BUG #2 (schematic-level, this leaf's own template): INA181's IN-
## (pin 4) was omitted from the GND net table entirely on the first pass --
## the re-derived netlist showed `unconnected-(U10x-IN--Pad4)`, a genuinely
## floating differential input on the trip-watch CSA (undefined/noisy
## amplifier output, not a cosmetic gap). Fixed by adding (U_INA,"4") to
## the leaf's GND net table, matching the platform's own sec6.13 INA181
## precedent (gen-modules.py ties SENSE_LO to amp pin 4) -- this design's
## shunt LO terminal is itself the GND node (2-terminal R_2512 land), so
## IN- reading GND directly is the correct simplification, not a shortcut.
## Caught by netlist re-derivation (grep for "unconnected-"), not by ERC
## (a fully undriven, un-netted pin with no net-table entry at all doesn't
## trip a "not driven" ERC class the way a mis-routed signal pin does --
## the SAME detection gap the 03-mcu GND-pin bug (addendum 5, item 1) hit).

## MECHANICAL BUG CLASS (same family as addendum 4's "hand-consumed point

## DESIGN DECISIONS made (not silently assumed, recorded per CLAUDE.md):
## (1) Shunt convention: 2-terminal R_Small on the R_2512 land (Kelvin taps
## in copper at layout), matching the platform's DOMINANT precedent (EPS/
## PCIe/24-pin RS1-3) rather than the rarer 24-pin-RS4 4-terminal
## CEC_SHUNT_4T exception (which pairs with a genuine Vishay WSK2512-family
## MPN, not the Bourns CSS2H-2512R-1L00F this leaf's BOM line names) --
## avoids inventing/flagging an unverified WSK2512 value+MPN for a part
## whose real Kelvin geometry is a PCB-layout-time concern either way.
## (2) Trip threshold: a LOCAL FIXED R-divider (R_TH1/R_TH2, +3V3/2), not a
## firmware-programmable PWM+RC DAC the way the platform's per-cable MODULE
## sec6.13 cell gets one -- 03-mcu's fixed 20-GPIO budget (HARD CONSTRAINT,
## zero spare) has no channel left for a dedicated per-loop threshold PWM.
## [wb] bench-calibrate the divider ratio per loop's expected shunt swing.
## (3) Loop de-gate mechanism: DEGATE_RAIL ANDs the RAW PWM (before the RC
## filter), not a hard gate-to-GND clamp -- since the FET is analog-driven
## (op-amp output), a "soft-kill" (0% duty -> a genuine 0V reference, the
## loop then regulates toward 0A) is the achievable mechanism; R_PD (a
## plain, always-present gate-to-GND pulldown, independent of DEGATE_RAIL)
## is the hard backup matching DESIGN-SHEET C.10's literal "gate pull-down
## at the gate pin" rule. Flagged as a DIFFERENT mechanism than banks/SCP's
## hard AND-gated drive (which IS a genuine kill, since THOSE gates are
## logic-level all the way to the FET) -- both reuse the SAME SN74AHCT1G08
## part, just wired into a different point in the signal chain.
## (4) PWM_SETPOINT_{rail}/DEGATE_RAIL/TRIP_LOOP_{rail}/TRIP_ANY/RAIL_{rail}
## are ALL `global_nets` (real KiCad global_label), never `hier_exports` --
## even though several are nominally 2-leaf nets today, global_nets avoids
## `build_thin_parent`'s PARENT_PINS bookkeeping entirely and matches
## 03-mcu's own already-committed convention for every one of these names
## it already owns. RAIL_{rail} deliberately does NOT cross the J_HARN
## harness connector (unlike the logic-level signals) -- it is real load
## current, not a "logic-level enable/PWM" signal, so per the split-arch
## rule it reaches the FET drains directly from 08-deck-io's own fixture
## connector (not yet built).
## (5) Ref-number allocation: fresh per-class counters (nref(), R/C/U from
## 100, Q/F from 1 -- Q and F are BRAND NEW ref classes, 01/02/03 never
## used them) shared across ALL of 04-08 so no leaf can collide with
## another by construction; verified clear of 01/02/03's own R1-R56/C1-C64/
## U1-U17/D1-D6/J1-J7/SW3-SW5/TH1-TH6 ranges.
